How to read this. Vehicles per household is an estimate: (road motor vehicles ÷ population) × average household size. “Vehicles” includes trucks and buses, so values overstate private cars. Years differ by country (vehicles 2012–2025, household size 2000–2023). Grey = no data (Oman and Laos were excluded as unreliable).
Sources: Wikipedia, “List of countries and territories by motor vehicles per capita” and “List of countries by number of households” (national statistics offices, UN, OICA).
